Photosynthesis reference

6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂

Light reactions: capture light energy, split water, release O₂, and generate ATP and NADPH.

Calvin cycle: uses ATP and NADPH to reduce carbon derived from CO₂ into carbohydrate precursors.

Limiting factor: the factor in shortest effective supply relative to demand. Raising a non-limiting factor may have little effect until the actual limitation is relieved.

Experimental caution: net CO₂ exchange reflects both photosynthesis and respiration.
